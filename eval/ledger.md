## Self-play batches

Train seeds are `run --seed 1 --start K`, test seeds the files of `eval/seeds/` (other bases), and the two never meet (the user, 2026-11-01): a self-play or fork job plays train seeds only, an eval job a test file only (`box.py` refuses anything else), and training refuses a list holding an eval job's runs (`common.train_lists`). `eval/seeds/retired/` holds what can no longer test: promotion-1-500, whose last 200 seeds' runs are in c7-1's and c7-2's data, and so in c8-1's and c8-2's line. promotion-1-300 and its parts, final-1 and a10-check-1 have never been trained on. A new batch takes the next free block of 100,000 seeds, entered here before
it starts: shard i starts at block + 1000·i and plays `--n 1000` at most, so no two shards share a seed.
