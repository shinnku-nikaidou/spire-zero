title: Weaknesses and what is not built
description: Where this AI is weak today, and the parts of the design that are not built yet.
---

The earlier chapters describe only what runs today. This chapter lists where the AI is weak and which parts are not built yet.

## Weaknesses

- **NoSL has not been trained.** The two networks learn only from SL runs. NoSL has no training of its own, no gate and no results, and the networks' values lean optimistic there.
- **Ascension 10 is uneven.** The champion wins three runs in five there, but the Fool won only 25 of 200, and the Necrobinder 104 of 200.
- **The networks have not learned the Dread mode.** They read the mode and its dominions, but none of the runs they learned from was played in it, so they value a Dread run as an ordinary one at Ascension 10. The champion won 197 of its 1,199 Dread runs.
- **Most lost runs die to a boss.** At Ascension 10 that is 373 of the 486 lost runs, two thirds of them at the act 3 boss.
- **What a decision does more than four floors later comes through another V.** That V learns from whole runs, where hundreds of decisions share one outcome, so a slow consequence it does not see, V does not learn either.
- **Exploration is mixed into the labels.** A random draw in a decision's next four floors lowers its label, the V that labels the rest learned from runs that explore, and V does not know how much a run explored.
- **Combat search is narrow.** SL keeps only 8 positions per turn, so a better line may be pushed out, and most plans are not guaranteed to be the best.
- **Inside each world, NoSL's search knows the future.** The flaw is corrected only for the first move, and the size of the rest has not been measured.
- **A combat is scored only by HP, max HP and potions.** Gold stolen or a card grown in a combat is not part of the score, and the HP left after an act's boss counts in full, though the next act opens with an Ancient who restores HP.
- **The map V reads has no links**, and it ends with this act.
- **A few rules of play are written by hand.** Most make up for V, which rates many potions below an empty slot. A potion reward is always taken while the belt has room, and a potion is thrown away only when the belt is full and a new potion is there to take. The Foul Potion, which hurts the player too in a fight, is never used in a fight and never thrown away; at a merchant it is thrown first, for the gold he pays, and a move into a shop counts that gold in advance. The relic in a treasure chest is always taken, never skipped.
- **The gate is on the loose side.** A new pair passes if it wins as few as 3 seeds that the old pair lost, while the old pair wins none that the new pair lost. How often the gate lets a pair through by mistake has not been measured.
- **Whether the simulator and the real game agree everywhere** has no full set of tests yet.

## Not built yet

- **Training, a gate and results for NoSL.**
- **Search that looks past the current room.** Today search stops at the room's exit in both environments.
- **A cheaper combat search.** Start by keeping 1 position per turn and widen round by round, so that easy combats stop early and hard ones get more.
- **Search results as labels.** Today the labels are the real outcomes of runs and, for V past four floors, the estimate of another V.
- **Playing a whole run alone with nobody present.** Today the player has to be present to start the AI playing for them.
- **Explanations.** Telling the player why a move was chosen.
