Every search stops somewhere. PIMC stops at the end of the turn; the SL planner usually reaches the end of the fight, but it too has to rank the states where its turns end before it knows how the fight goes on ([The SL planner: beam §1](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-1)). The state where a search stops is a **leaf**, and the function that says what it is worth is the **leaf value**. In one line: the leaf is the net's distribution of endings, each priced by V, the macro brain's chance to win the run, on the run that ending would leave.

## 1. What a leaf is

A leaf is either a state at the start of a turn, after the enemies' turn has been played for real, or the end of the fight, won or lost. Both are valued by the same function, `ICombatLeaf.Value(status, observation, run, history)` (`src/Sts2Ai.Brain/Combat/CombatLeaf.cs`): a lost fight is worth nothing, a won one by what it leaves the run, and a fight in progress by what it is expected to leave. `ICombatLeaf.Outcome` gives, from the same evaluation, the value with how the fight is expected to end: the chance to win it and the HP held at a win ([§5](#sec-5)).

- **Observation and run.** The net reads what the player sees, plus the public record of the enemies' moves so far ([Prompts, moves and workers §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/shared/#sec-2)); the prices read the run around the fight as it stands at the leaf ([§2](#sec-2)). Neither reads the draw order or a random stream, so the leaf cannot leak hidden state, in either environment.
- **Ordering, more than level.** A search uses the leaf to compare lines: what matters is that a better state gets a higher value.

## 2. The price of an ending: V

No number in the leaf is set by hand. How a fight ends matters for what it leaves the run, and the run already has a value: V, the macro brain's chance to win the run from a run view ([Macro brain: the network](/en/arch/macro-brain/network/)). So an ending is priced by V of the run that ending would leave:

- **A lost fight** is 0: the run is over.
- **A won fight that is over** is V of its run as it stands: the HP held, the potions left, max HP and gold as they are, the deck with what the fight changed for good (a card grown, a card stolen), the relics, the map ahead (`CombatNet.Outcome`, `src/Sts2Ai.Brain/Combat/CombatNet.cs`). Every finished line, and so every plan's final score, is V of the run its fight leaves.
- **A fight in progress** is the net's endings, each priced by V of the run at that ending's HP and with the potions it keeps ([§5](#sec-5)).

**The run at a leaf** is the world's (`ICombatWorld.Run`, `src/Sts2Ai.Protocol/World.cs`). In SL the world is the node's clone, and its run is the live game's run view (GameBridge's `Observe.Run`, without upgrade previews): what the fight has done to the deck, the relics, the potions, HP, max HP and gold is there as the game holds it. In NoSL a world is a fight built from the observation, whose own run is neither the real deck nor the real map; it takes the run around the fight that the decision's request carries (`PimcWorldRequest.Run`) and puts in it the built fight's relics, potions, HP, max HP and gold (`SimWorlds`, `src/Sts2Ai.SimWorker/Simulation/SimWorlds.cs`).

V reads more of the run than the combat net reads of the fight: the cards a relic keeps out of the fight (Pael's Tooth's) are no input of the combat net, and reach the leaf through V's prices alone ([Macro brain: the value network §2](/en/arch/macro-brain/network/#sec-2)).

So the leaf needs both nets: `pimc` and `plan` need `--combat-net`, and `--combat-net` needs `--value`, the V that prices its endings (`LeafChoice`, `src/Sts2Ai.SimWorker/Brains/LeafChoice.cs`). The champion plays the same V in its leaves as in its macro decisions.

## 3. The combat net

For a fight still in progress the leaf asks the combat net. It reads a round's start and predicts how the fight will end under the policy that played its training games. Its inputs and layers are in [The combat net](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/), a real state followed through it is in [One state through the net](/en/arch/combat-brain/walkthrough/), and its training is in [Training the combat net](/en/arch/learning/combat-net/).

`--combat-net FILE` gives it to the search: to the planner (shown as `plan NAME`) or to PIMC (`pimc NAME`).

## 4. The two outputs

**The endings.** 51 chances: a death, or one of 50 buckets of the share of max HP still to be lost from this state to the end of a won fight, from −0.25 (a fight that heals) to 1. The buckets the HP held rules out are closed, with a chance of exactly 0 ([The combat net §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-7)).

**The potions.** After a win, each held potion's chance of being gone, one for each of 5 groups of 10 buckets in a row: a win that costs little HP and one that costs much can drink different potions ([The combat net §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-7)).

The figure shows them on the 16 round starts the net file carries, real states from held-out runs; loading the net checks that it gives these outputs, as the trainer computed them. The shaded bands are the closed buckets. A potion's chance shown is its chance at a win, each group's chance weighted by the chances of its buckets. Weights: `weights/all/c9-1.json`.

## 5. Pricing the endings

A distribution is not yet a number. The leaf gives each ending its price from [§2](#sec-2). For HP share $s$ now (HP over max HP), the chance $p_0$ of a death, $p_k$ of bucket $k$ with middle $m_k$, the potions held $H$, and the chance $u_{i,g}$ that held potion $i$ is gone after a win ending in group $g$, where the buckets go 10 in a row into 5 groups and bucket $k$ is in group $g(k) = \lceil k/10 \rceil$:

$$
\text{value} = \sum_{k=1}^{50} p_k \sum_{S \subseteq H} \Big(\prod_{i \in S} (1 - u_{i,g(k)}) \prod_{i \in H \setminus S} u_{i,g(k)}\Big)\, V\big(\text{run}_{h_k,\,S}\big),
\qquad h_k = \operatorname{clamp}\big(\operatorname{round}((s - m_k)\times\text{max HP}),\ 1,\ \text{max HP}\big)
$$

where $\text{run}_{h, S}$ is the leaf's run with $h$ HP holding the potions of $S$, each in its own slot, the others' slots empty (`CombatNet.Outcome`). A death adds nothing, since it is worth 0. A closed bucket's chance is exactly 0 and asks nothing of V; an open one asks V once for each subset of the potions held. V's answers are kept by the run without its HP and potions, the HP and the potions, so the many leaves of one search that share a run ask V once for each HP and potions they reach. Term by term, on a real state, in [One state through the net §13](/en/arch/combat-brain/walkthrough/#sec-13).

The form is **what the state holds, exactly, then what the net says is still to come, as V sees it**. $s$ enters exactly: a bucket is a share still to be lost from the HP held now, so states of one beam differ by their true HP before the net says anything. A potion drunk is gone from every subset, and V prices the run without it. Each bucket weighs the potions by its own group's chances, so a costly win, which may drink a potion, and a cheap one, which may keep it, are priced each with its own potions.

**No state caps its lines.** A win's worth is V of the run it leaves, and a line can still raise that past winning now with what the state holds: max HP or gold gained in the fight, a card grown, a potion drunk that V rates below an empty slot. So no value of a state bounds what its lines can reach, and the planner prunes nothing by value ([The SL planner: beam §2](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-2)).

**How the fight ends.** The same call gives two numbers a player reads (`LeafOutcome`). The chance to win is $1 - p_0$. The HP held at a win is the HP share left after each win bucket, averaged over the win buckets and kept between 0 and 1, times max HP:

$$
\text{hp} = \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{50} p_k\,(s - m_k)}{1 - p_0},\ 0,\ 1\right) \times \text{max HP}.
$$

A fight that is over has its own: a win 1 and the HP held, a loss 0 and 0. The SL planner keeps both for each line, and the mod shows them for its options ([The SL planner: beam §5](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-5)). A leaf that predicts no ending gives a fight still going no chance to win. Neither number changes a search: the value alone ranks states.

**Where the net acts.** A finished line is V of its run alone. The net decides what a fight still going is worth: the beam's order and what it keeps, the tie among losing lines, a line stopped at the turn cap, and in PIMC every leaf at the end of the turn.

## 6. One net, two environments

SL and NoSL play the same net file, and the same V. The net learned from SL's games, so its endings are those of the planner, which sees what NoSL's search must guess and wins more from the same state. In NoSL the same state is therefore rated by a stronger player's outcomes.

## 7. What this value cannot see

These follow from the two heads, their labels and the prices.

- **Potions by group, each on its own.** A potion's chance follows the HP lost only group by group: within a group of 10 buckets the leaf takes the bucket and the potion's use as independent. And each potion's chance is taken apart from the others': two potions that are drunk together or not at all are priced as if each went on its own.
- **What is still to come, past HP and potions.** The net predicts the HP and the potions a fight ends with; the rest of the run is priced as it stands at the leaf. Gold an enemy will still steal, max HP still to be lost, a card still to grow in the turns to come are in no price until they happen; once they have, V reads them.
- **A bucket's width.** An ending's HP is its bucket's middle: HP within one bucket, a share of 0.025 of max HP, is priced alike.
- **V's own view.** Every price is V's: what V misreads about a run, the leaf misreads about a fight: a potion V rates below an empty slot is one the search will drink for nothing. In NoSL the world's run keeps the decision's deck, so what the fight does to the deck shows in SL only.
- **Siblings.** The logs hold one ending per fight, so no label separates two states a search compares within one round. What separates them is HP held, counted in full, and what the net learned across fights.
- **Its own player.** The endings are those of the policy that played the training games: earlier nets, exploring, at the medium budget that also plays gates ([Training the combat net §3](/en/arch/learning/combat-net/#sec-3)).
