The state is a round start from the held-out test states in the champion's combat net file (`weights/all/c9-2.json`, `tests[11]`). Loading the net checks that it gives this state exactly these outputs ([The combat net §9](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-9)). The numbers come from `docs/scripts/combat-walk.py`, which stops if its layers do not end in the file's outputs.

## 10. The endings head: 51 logits

One linear layer gives 51 raw numbers. The first is the death's, {walk.head.death_logit}. A logit has no meaning alone: only the softmax in the next step, which compares them, makes them chances.

## 11. The mask, then the softmax

The HP share is {walk.ended.share}. A bucket $[l, l + 0.025)$ stays open if $l < 0.521$ and $l + 0.025 > 0.521 - 1$. Here:

- The bottom: a fight cannot heal more than the HP missing. {walk.state.max_hp - walk.state.hp} of {walk.state.max_hp} HP is a share of 0.479, more than the 0.25 the lowest bucket heals, so no bucket is closed at the bottom: the open ones start at {walk.head.open_from}.
- The top: a win cannot lose all the HP held, so the buckets from {walk.head.open_to} up are closed.

That leaves **{walk.head.open} of the 50 buckets open**. The others get a logit of $-10^4$ (`Net.reachable`, `learn/combat/net.py:223-229`), and the softmax gives them exactly 0.

The softmax over the rest gives the chances. A death has {walk.head.death}. The most likely buckets:

| {walk.head.tops[0].low} to {walk.head.tops[0].high} | {(walk.head.tops[0].low * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} to {(walk.head.tops[0].high * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} | {pct(walk.head.tops[0].chance)} |
| {walk.head.tops[1].low} to {walk.head.tops[1].high} | {(walk.head.tops[1].low * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} to {(walk.head.tops[1].high * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} | {pct(walk.head.tops[1].chance)} |
| {walk.head.tops[2].low} to {walk.head.tops[2].high} | {(walk.head.tops[2].low * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} to {(walk.head.tops[2].high * walk.state.max_hp).toFixed(1)} | {pct(walk.head.tops[2].chance)} |

The net expects this fight to be won at a small cost: {pct(walk.head.chances.slice(11, 21).reduce((a, b) => a + b, 0))} of the chance falls on buckets 11 to 20, a loss of 0 to 0.25 of max HP (up to 18 HP), and only {pct(walk.head.chances.slice(1, 11).reduce((a, b) => a + b, 0))} on a gain. ## 12. The potion head

The same 512 numbers go through a second linear layer, of 512 → {walk.potion.groups} × {walk.potion.outputs}: for each of {walk.potion.groups} groups of {walk.potion.per_group} buckets in a row, one output per entry of the potion table ([The combat net §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-7)). The likeliest win, a share of {walk.head.tops[0].low} to {walk.head.tops[0].high} still to be lost, is bucket {walk.head.tops[0].bucket}, in group {walk.potion.group + 1}, buckets {walk.potion.group * walk.potion.per_group + 1} to {(walk.potion.group + 1) * walk.potion.per_group}; the chances here are that group's. Fairy in a Bottle is entry {walk.potion.entry}. Its logit is {walk.potion.logit}, and the sigmoid makes that **{walk.potion.chance}**: the net expects the potion to be still held after most wins, and gone after about one in eleven. In this file the five groups hold the same weights ([The combat net §8](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-8)), so every group gives it the same chance. The player never drinks this potion; the game uses it on its own when the player would die, and brings the player back with 30 % of max HP.

Of each group's {walk.potion.outputs} outputs only the held potion's is read; the other {walk.potion.outputs - 1} are computed and not read. The largest of them here are {walk.potion.most_likely[0].id} and {walk.potion.most_likely[1].id}, at {walk.potion.most_likely[0].chance} and {walk.potion.most_likely[1].chance}: this state holds neither, so they mean nothing and train nothing. A potion that no training held, such as {walk.potion.unheld}, has `<unk>`'s row and shows its chance, {walk.potion.unk}.

## 13. The number the search reads

The search does not read the 51 chances or the potion's: it reads one number, the leaf value ([The leaf §5](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-5)). `CombatNet.Outcome` (`src/Sts2Ai.Brain/Combat/CombatNet.cs`) makes it by pricing every ending the net still allows with V, the macro brain's chance to win the run, on the run that ending would leave:

$$
\text{value} = \sum_{k=1}^{50} p_k \sum_{S \subseteq H} \Big(\prod_{i \in S} (1 - u_{i,g(k)}) \prod_{i \in H \setminus S} u_{i,g(k)}\Big)\, V\big(\text{the run at } h_k \text{ HP, holding } S\big)
$$

Here $H$ is the potions held, $u_{i,g}$ the potion head's chance that potion $i$ is gone after a win ending in group $g$, $g(k) = \lceil k/10 \rceil$ the group of bucket $k$, and $h_k$ the HP the player holds after bucket $k$: the HP share now less the bucket's middle, times max HP, kept between 1 and max HP.

For this state, $H$ is Fairy in a Bottle alone, so each open bucket gives two runs: with the potion, at a chance of $1 - u_{i,g(k)}$, {(1 - walk.potion.chance).toFixed(3)} in every group ([§12](#sec-12)), and without it. The {walk.head.open} open buckets make {2 * walk.head.open} runs for V, and a closed bucket, whose chance is exactly 0, makes none. The rest of each run is the run around the fight as it stands: the deck, the relics, max HP, gold, the map ahead. The file's test states hold a round start and no run, so this page cannot give V's numbers or the value; what the same call returns beside the value does not need them ([The leaf §5](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-5)):

| | number |
|---|---|
| chance to win, $1 - p_0$ | {walk.ended.win} |
| HP held at a win, averaged over the win buckets | {walk.ended.hp_at_win} of {walk.state.max_hp} |
| potions expected to be kept at a win, $\sum_{i \in H} (1 - \bar u_i)$ | {walk.ended.kept} |

Here $\bar u_i = \sum_{k=1}^{50} p_k\, u_{i,g(k)} / (1 - p_0)$, potion $i$'s chance of being gone at a win, each bucket's group's chance weighted by the bucket's.

So V is asked about runs that leave this fight with {walk.ended.hp_at_win} of {walk.state.max_hp} HP on average, most of them still holding the potion, and the value is V's average over those runs, weighted by their chances. Whether that is worth more than another state's ending is V's to say, from everything it reads of the run.

## 14. What a search sees

A search compares states. Change one thing at a time and run the net again:

| state | HP share | chance of a death | the likeliest bucket | HP held at a win |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as it is | {walk.ended.share} | {pct(walk.head.death)} | {walk.head.tops[0].low} to {walk.head.tops[0].high}, {pct(walk.head.tops[0].chance)} | {walk.ended.hp_at_win} |
| {walk.lost_hp} HP less | {walk.siblings.less_hp.share} | {pct(walk.siblings.less_hp.death)} | {walk.siblings.less_hp.tops[0].low} to {walk.siblings.less_hp.tops[0].high}, {pct(walk.siblings.less_hp.tops[0].chance)} | {walk.siblings.less_hp.hp_at_win} |
| the potion gone | {walk.siblings.no_potion.share} | {pct(walk.siblings.no_potion.death)} | {walk.siblings.no_potion.tops[0].low} to {walk.siblings.no_potion.tops[0].high}, {pct(walk.siblings.no_potion.tops[0].chance)} | {walk.siblings.no_potion.hp_at_win} |

With {walk.lost_hp} HP less the likeliest buckets stay the same and grow a little, and a death goes from {pct(walk.head.death)} to {pct(walk.siblings.less_hp.death)}. The buckets are shares still to be lost from the HP held now, so the HP held at a win falls, from {walk.ended.hp_at_win} to {walk.siblings.less_hp.hp_at_win}, though by less than the {walk.lost_hp} HP taken away: from the lower HP the net expects a win to cost {(walk.state.hp - walk.lost_hp - walk.siblings.less_hp.hp_at_win).toFixed(1)} HP, against {(walk.state.hp - walk.ended.hp_at_win).toFixed(1)} now. The HP held counts in full ([The combat net §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-7)), and V prices every ending of this state at less HP.

Without the potion a death goes from {pct(walk.head.death)} to {pct(walk.siblings.no_potion.death)}, the likeliest bucket's chance from {pct(walk.head.tops[0].chance)} to {pct(walk.siblings.no_potion.tops[0].chance)}, and the HP held at a win from {walk.ended.hp_at_win} to {walk.siblings.no_potion.hp_at_win}. Fairy in a Bottle acts only when the player would die; nothing here says why the net moves the other endings. The difference between the two states is then mostly V's: the same HP, one run holding the potion and one not. What a potion is worth is not a number set for the fight but what V makes of holding it in this run.

## 16. The label

So far the net has predicted. In training this state also has an answer, from how its fight really ended. The file does not keep that ending, so take one for illustration: **suppose the fight is won at {walk.label.end_hp} of {walk.state.max_hp} HP, with the potion still held**.

- **The ending's class.** The share lost from this state is {walk.state.hp}/{walk.state.max_hp} − {walk.label.end_hp}/{walk.state.max_hp} = {walk.label.lost}, in the bucket from {walk.label.low} to {walk.label.high}: class {walk.label.class}. Every round start of that fight would get its own class, from its own HP.
- **The potion's label.** It is still in the first run view after the fight, so its label is 0. Class {walk.label.class} is in group {Math.floor((walk.label.class - 1) / walk.potion.per_group) + 1}, so the label trains that group's chance alone.
- **The loss.** The chance of class {walk.label.class} was {walk.label.chance}, so the endings' cross-entropy is {walk.label.ending_loss}. The potion's, by that group's chance, is {walk.label.potion_loss}. The sample's loss is their sum, and a gradient step would push that bucket's chance up.

The ending's loss is the larger: the net spreads its chance over many buckets, so no single class gets much of it, while the potion it expected to be kept was kept. How labels are made across a whole list of runs is in [Training the combat net §3](/en/arch/learning/combat-net/#sec-3).
