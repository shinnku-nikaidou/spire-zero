PIMC's budget is two numbers, `Budget(Worlds, NodesPerWorld)`: $K$ worlds, each searched with a number of nodes, 8 × 300 by default, where a node is one action played ([§1](#sec-1)). Nothing else sets it: a plan is followed without a search only when it repeats a cycle ([§2](#sec-2)), a world's nodes go to the first actions evenly ([§3](#sec-3)), and every room gets the same budget ([§4](#sec-4)).

## 1. Worlds and nodes

In each world the nodes are split evenly over the first actions, so each first action gets a fair look; inside a subtree, once its share is spent, the state is scored as ending the turn ([Search §1](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-1)). On one core a decision costs about 0.55–0.75 s; the worlds are independent, so the worker pool searches one world per worker, and with 8 workers an action takes about 70 ms, with the same result ([§5](#sec-5)).

How large $K$ should be is a question of returns. Each world adds a sample to every first action's mean, so the noise falls as $1/\sqrt{K}$, but the bias of [Search §4](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-4)–[§5](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-5) does not fall at all. Elsewhere: in LoCM, a card-game study, PIMC saturates beyond 32 worlds; in ytcs, a prior-art STS2 project, $K$ = 64 gained 0.018. Here $K$ and the nodes per world are 8 × 300 for every fight, and nothing has tuned them yet. As rough numbers, 1–2 s per decision on 8 workers gives about 5,000 branches; at $K$ = 16 that is about 300 per world, enough to enumerate one turn with transpositions merged.

## 2. Following the plan

PIMC searches again before every action, with one exception. When the best line repeats a cycle ([Search §6](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-6)), and every world plans the same rest of the turn and expects the same observation before each of its steps, the plan is played without searching again, one step at a time, for as long as the fight shows what the worlds expected (`TurnPlan`, [Combat brain §4](/en/arch/combat-brain/#sec-4)). A draw, a random result or a disagreement between the worlds sends the decision back to search.

Following every plan the worlds agree on, not only a cycle's, saves time but did not pass its gate. On the first 6 act 1 panel fights, 66 of 142 decisions went without a search, every action the same, in 27% less time; in whole runs of the champion on 16 train seeds, a run took 31% less time. Its not-worse gate ([Evaluation: gates §7](/en/arch/evaluation/gates/#sec-7)) was still open after all 200 seeds, −0.72 ± 0.92 floors with 126 seeds to the same floor.

## 3. One share per first action

Each world's $N$ nodes are split evenly over its $m$ first actions: each gets $\max(1, \lfloor N/m \rfloor)$ (`PimcBrain.Root`), and a share left unspent is not passed on. Inside its subtree a first action's search goes depth first until the share is spent, and the state there is scored as ending the turn, the option tried first ([Search §1](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-1)). A first action that raises a card prompt shares its share evenly with the prompt's other answers, each a line of the action, a part left unspent passing on to the next answer ([Search §1](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-1)).

An even split gives a hopeless first action as many nodes as the best one. Spending them where the choice is made is open ([§7](#sec-7)).

## 4. The same budget in every room

Elite and boss fights decide runs, so they could get more search. The evidence elsewhere is strong: in spire-mentor, raising the boss budget from 1,000 to 4,000 lifted the act 1 clear rate from 21.5% to over 65%. Here twice the nodes per world in boss fights failed its gate on seeds 0–99 (2026-10-28): 90 of the 100 pairs tied, the mean difference was −0.39 ± 0.32 floors, 43 runs passed the act 1 boss against the champion's 45, and runs took 15% longer. So every room gets 8 × 300; four times the nodes is untried.

## 5. Workers

Each world of a decision is one request to the worker pool (`PimcWorldRequest`, [Combat brain: prompts, moves and workers §3](/en/arch/combat-brain/shared/#sec-3)): the worker builds the fight from the observation with the build seed, takes world $k$ of the worlds drawn from the search seed, searches this turn and returns the best line after each first action. `PooledPimc` sends one request per world and merges the replies in world order. On 6 act 1 fights, 8 workers take 68.7 ms per action against 307.5 ms in one process, with the same moves.

PIMC plays in NoSL only: SL's one exact world is the planner's ([The SL planner](/en/arch/sl-planner/)), and a champion that plays `pimc` refuses an SL run (`src/Sts2Ai.SimWorker/Brains/ChampionOptions.cs:40`).

## 7. Weaknesses and open questions

1. **$K$ and the nodes per world.** 8 × 300 for every fight, untuned, split evenly over the first actions ([§3](#sec-3)). A fixed set of fights can show the marginal gain in HP per added world ([§1](#sec-1)).
2. **How large fusion is.** It has had one small measurement ([Search §4](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-4)): 300–500 decisions rich in reveals would decide whether ISMCTS is worth its cost.
3. **The leaf at the end of the turn.** The search sees the fight past this turn only through the leaf, so the leaf's gaps are the search's ([Combat brain: the leaf §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-7)).
4. **The sampler.** Monsters' queued moves are drawn uniformly among those that match the intent, and hidden counters have no posterior ([Search §5](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-5)).
5. **Prompts in each world.** A prompt's answer is chosen after the world's own draws, which widens strategy fusion by an amount not measured ([Search §4](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-4)).
6. **No report of the current champion** in NoSL, and its combat net learned from SL's games and has never been trained or gated in NoSL ([Combat brain: the leaf §6](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-6)).

## 8. The budget on TurnEnds <Badge text="planned" variant="caution" />

The planned budget stays the two numbers of [§1](#sec-1). What the planned search changes is how a world's nodes are spent: the merged `TurnEnds` ([Search §7](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-7)) does not give each first action its own share, as [§3](#sec-3) does.

**What stays and must.** `MoveRecord` stays for building worlds even if the net stops reading it: it is what lifts the worlds' fidelity from 82–89% to 96–98% on hallway fights ([Overview §3](/en/arch/pimc/#sec-3)).

**Conditions.** The merged `TurnEnds` needs a NoSL gate ([Search §7](/en/arch/pimc/search/#sec-7)).
