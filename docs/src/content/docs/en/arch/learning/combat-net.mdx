The combat net is the combat search's learned leaf: from a round's start it predicts how the fight will end under the policy that played its training games. What it reads and how its layers combine that are in [The combat net](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/), and what the search does with its outputs is in [The leaf](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/). This page is about how it is trained, from which data, and what the trained net measures.

## 1. What it predicts

From a round's start, two things:

- **The ending**: a distribution over 51 classes, a death or one of 50 buckets of the share of max HP still to be lost from this state, the buckets the HP held rules out closed.
- **The potions**: after a win, each held potion's chance of being gone.

`learn/combat/` trains it (`combat.train`, `learn/combat/train.py:40`). `--combat-net FILE` plays it, in the SL planner (shown as `plan NAME`) or in PIMC (`pimc NAME`).

## 2. What it reads

Everything is in [The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2). `learn/combat/encode.py` is the one specification, which `CombatNet` follows in C#.

## 3. Samples and labels

`learn/combat/data.py` makes the samples from a list of decision logs and run records, of every character ([Reference §4](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-4)).

- **A sample is a round's start**: the first combat decision of each round in the logs, the state a search leaf stands in, since the search's leaves are the starts of the next round ([The leaf §1](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-1)). A combat's decisions close at the next macro decision, or when the round goes back; card-prompt lines are skipped (`combats`, `learn/combat/data.py:33-67`).
- **The enemies' history** before it is rebuilt from the earlier rounds' starts, each living enemy's intents by its uid, as Harness's `Dispatcher` builds it (`witness`, `learn/combat/data.py:70-77`).
- **The ending's label is relative to the state**: a death, or the bucket of the HP share at this state less the share at the fight's end (`ending`, `learn/combat/data.py:97-102`). A heal larger than the lowest bucket falls into it. So every round start of a fight has one end, but its own label. Monte Carlo: it is what really happened under the policy that played ([The loop §3](/en/arch/learning/loop/#sec-3)).
- **A potion's label** comes from the first run view after the fight, per copy: a potion held at this state and absent there is gone (`potions_used`, `learn/combat/data.py:80-90`). Not from the actions, which miss a Fairy in a Bottle spent with no action, 6.2 % of all potions gone. Not from the last round's start, which misses a potion drunk at the last action. A potion gained later in the fight is no potion of this state. A death trains only the ending.
- **What is left out** (`read`, `learn/combat/data.py:131-157`): failed runs; a combat with no `end` in its record, unless it is a lost run's last, which counts as a death; a win with no run view after it; and every run whose logged combats do not match its record's, in number or in encounters, which the trainer counts and reports.
- **One environment.** `--env SL|NoSL` reads only that environment's lines; a log line of the other fails the training. The net is trained on `--env SL` ([The leaf §6](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-6)).
- **Weights and split.** Every round start weighs the same, and every fifth run by run id is held out, with all its round starts, for early stopping and the report. One held-out round start in 1,000 keeps its observation and history for the net file's test states (`KEEP`).

**The data** the champion's weights last learned from is whole SL self-play at ascension 10, the 10,038 runs of `v8g2`, every combat fought at the medium budget. That makes 736,075 round starts, of which 153,718 are held out. Self-play's seeds and the gates' never meet, and `--list` refuses a list holding an eval job's runs (`common.train_lists`). c8-3 went on from c8-2, which learned from the 8,087 A10 runs of `v8g1` and went on from c8-1, whose weights learned at ascension 0: 21,263 self-play runs, most of them at the low budget ([The SL planner: budget §2](/en/arch/sl-planner/budget/#sec-2)), plus 600 runs at medium budget on the seeds `promotion-301-500`, which is why `promotion-1-500` no longer serves as a test set ([Evaluation: metrics, seeds and reports §5](/en/arch/evaluation/metrics/#sec-5)).

On the set `c7` of those weights, of the wins' round starts, the mask never closes the true bucket, and 98 of 1,479,991 heal beyond the lowest bucket. 9.4 % of the round starts hold a potion.

## 4. Loss and training

```bash
uv run python -m combat.train --list data/silent/lists/v8g2 --env SL --name NAME --test-list data/silent/lists/medium
```

The loss of a sample is (`loss`, `learn/combat/net.py:326-333`)

$$
\text{loss} = \text{CE}(\text{masked endings}, \text{class}) + \text{won} \sum_{\text{held}} \text{BCE}(u_{\text{id}}, \text{gone}).
$$

The potions' term has weight 1 (`POTION_WEIGHT`), which is about 1 % of the loss: 0.051 potion rows per won sample at about 0.33 nats, against a cross-entropy of about 1.24. So the body stays the endings'.

The settings are fixed in the code (`learn/combat/train.py:34-36`): AdamW at learning rate 10⁻³ and weight decay 10⁻⁴; batches of 256; at most 20 epochs, ending after 3 without a held-out loss lower by 10⁻⁴. An experiment changes the code, and only a result keeps the change. The token tables are the game's, fixed ([The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2)).

**Going on from a net (`--init FILE`).** Training can start from a net file's weights, read back from its ONNX graph (`export.weights`), at the lower learning rate 3 × 10⁻⁴. The file's weights stand unless an epoch beats their held-out loss. A file with other tables than the game's is refused. c8-3 went on from c8-2 this way, on the 10,038 runs of `v8g2` alone: its best epoch was 2 of 6, and the held-out loss fell from c8-2's 1.2913 to 1.2579.

**The report** (`learn/combat/report.py`), one JSON line on stdout, also kept as the net file's `fit`: `env`, `runs`, `held_out_runs`, `samples`, `held_out_samples`, `vocab`, then `held_out` and the same by act, room, encounter, HP (below 30 % or not) and potion id. With `--test-list`, `medium` holds the same for that whole list, which is reported and never trained on.

| field | what it says |
|---|---|
| `ce`, `ce_prior` | the endings' cross-entropy, and that of the training set's frequencies |
| `death`, `predicted_death` | the means, actual and predicted |
| `rank_within`, `rank_within_share` | rank correlation with the end's price within each (encounter, round) of 10 or more, by their sizes; beside it, the HP share's |
| `rank_lost`, `rank_lost_share` | the same with the HP lost, on wins: what a net adds to the HP held |
| `auc_death`, `auc_death_share` | the chance that a death's round start scores above a survivor's within each (encounter, round) with 3 or more of each; beside it, the HP share's |
| `potions` | `rows`, `used`, `predicted`, `log_loss`, and `log_loss_id` and `log_loss_id_room`, those of a rate per id and per id and room |

**The net file** goes to `weights/all/NAME.json` and is never overwritten ([Reference §6](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-6)). Its fields are in [Reference §7](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-7).

## 5. What it measures

The numbers are the report in `weights/all/c9-1.json`. "Held out" is every fifth run of the training list, 6,004 runs of all five characters at ascension 0 with 407,042 round starts, played at the low budget.

| metric | held out | the HP share alone |
|---|---|---|
| endings' cross-entropy (the prior's) | 1.249 (2.352) | – |
| deaths, actual and predicted | 9.27 %, 10.03 % | – |
| rank with the end's price | 0.929 | 0.778 |
| rank of HP lost, on wins | 0.727 | 0.121 |
| AUC of a death | 0.957 | 0.679 |
| potions' log loss (per id and room) | 0.340 (0.564) | – |

**What to read.** The rank with the end's price says little: the HP share alone gets most of it, since the end's price follows the HP held. The rank of HP lost and the AUC of a death are what the net adds beyond the HP held.

By room, on the held-out runs:

| room | round starts | cross-entropy (prior) | deaths, actual and predicted | rank of HP lost | AUC of a death (HP share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hallway | 264,191 | 0.988 (1.958) | 0.9 %, 1.0 % | 0.702 | 0.973 (0.848) |
| elite | 66,949 | 1.786 (2.955) | 9.6 %, 10.1 % | 0.784 | 0.968 (0.728) |
| boss | 75,902 | 1.684 (3.191) | 38.2 %, 41.4 % | 0.792 | 0.953 (0.655) |

**Low HP**, below 30 % of max HP, where 27.3 % of the held-out round starts die: the AUC of a death is 0.945 against the HP share's 0.519.

**The potion head by id.** On the held-out runs Fairy in a Bottle is used 17.7 % of the time and predicted at 18.9 %, with a log loss of 0.155 against 0.381 per id and room. Across all rows, 31.2 % are used, and 32.1 % predicted.

**The hardest fight.** On Knowledge Demon, where 53.2 % of the held-out round starts die, the net predicts 55.3 %, and its AUC of a death is 0.970 against 0.675. Its rank of HP lost, on wins, is 0.799 against 0.116.

**Against the net it went on from.** On the same held-out round starts c9-0, which had learned from the Silent's runs alone, has a loss of 4.33 against c9-1's 1.27. The gap is the other characters': the Silent's round starts go from 1.27 to 1.20, the Ironclad's from 9.72 to 1.54, the Regent's from 5.87 to 1.23, the Necrobinder's from 4.34 to 1.13 and the Defect's from 4.17 to 1.30.

Which of these translates to more won runs is for a gate to say ([Evaluation: gates §5](/en/arch/evaluation/gates/#sec-5)). With V v9-1, at ascension 0 and medium, it won 46, 35, 31 and 9 of 100 runs with the Ironclad, the Regent, the Defect and the Necrobinder, where v9-0 with c9-0 won 1, 2, 1 and 0, and 74 with the Silent against 86 ([Evaluation: champions and baselines §2](/en/arch/evaluation/champions/#sec-2)).
