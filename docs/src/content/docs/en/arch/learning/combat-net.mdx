The combat net is the combat search's learned leaf: from a round's start it predicts how the fight will end under the policy that played its training games. What it reads and how its layers combine that are in [The combat net](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/), and what the search does with its outputs is in [The leaf](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/). This page is about how it is trained, from which data, and what the trained net measures.

## 1. What it predicts

From a round's start, two things:

- **The ending**: a distribution over 51 classes, a death or one of 50 buckets of the share of max HP still to be lost from this state, the buckets the HP held rules out closed.
- **The potions**: after a win, each held potion's chance of being gone, one for each of 5 groups of 10 buckets in a row, so that a costly win can drink other potions than a cheap one ([The combat net §7](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-7)).

`learn/combat/` trains it (`combat.train`, `learn/combat/train.py:40`). `--combat-net FILE` plays it, in the SL planner (shown as `plan NAME`) or in PIMC (`pimc NAME`).

## 2. What it reads

Everything is in [The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2). `learn/combat/encode.py` is the one specification, which `CombatNet` follows in C#.

## 3. Samples and labels

`learn/combat/data.py` makes the samples from a list of decision logs and run records, of every character ([Reference §4](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-4)).

- **A sample is a round's start**: the first combat decision of each round in the logs, the state a search leaf stands in, since the search's leaves are the starts of the next round ([The leaf §1](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-1)). A combat's decisions close at the next macro decision, or when the round goes back; card-prompt lines are skipped (`combats`, `learn/combat/data.py:39-73`). A fight the auto-win rule won at its start asked no decision, so it gives no sample; its run's elites and bosses do, the same as in a whole run ([Simulator: whole runs §8](/en/arch/simulator/runs/#sec-8)).
- **The enemies' history** before it is rebuilt from the earlier rounds' starts, each living enemy's intents by its uid, as Harness's `Dispatcher` builds it (`witness`, `learn/combat/data.py:76-83`).
- **The ending's label is relative to the state**: a death, or the bucket of the HP share at this state less the share at the fight's end (`ending`, `learn/combat/data.py:103-108`). A heal larger than the lowest bucket falls into it. So every round start of a fight has one end, but its own label. Monte Carlo: it is what really happened under the policy that played ([The loop §3](/en/arch/learning/loop/#sec-3)).
- **A potion's label** comes from the first run view after the fight, per copy: a potion held at this state and absent there is gone (`potions_used`, `learn/combat/data.py:86-96`). Not from the actions, which miss a Fairy in a Bottle spent with no action. Not from the last round's start, which misses a potion drunk at the last action. A potion gained later in the fight is no potion of this state. A label trains the potion's chance in the group of buckets the win ended in ([§4](#sec-4)). A death trains only the ending.
- **What is left out** (`read`, `learn/combat/data.py:131-157`): failed runs; a combat with no `end` in its record, unless it is a lost run's last, which counts as a death; a win with no run view after it; and every run whose logged combats do not match its record's, in number or in encounters, which the trainer counts and reports.
- **One environment.** `--env SL|NoSL` reads only that environment's lines; a log line of the other fails the training. The net is trained on `--env SL` ([The leaf §6](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-6)).
- **Weights and split.** Every round start weighs the same, and every fifth run by run id is held out, with all its round starts, for early stopping and the report. One held-out round start in 1,000 keeps its observation and history for the net file's test states (`KEEP`).

**The data** the champion's combat net, `weights/all/c9-2.json`, learned from is whole SL self-play of all five characters at ascension 0, every combat fought at the medium budget: 21,272 runs, which make 2,118,826 round starts, of which 430,142, from 4,310 runs, are held out. Self-play's seeds and the gates' never meet, and `--list` refuses a list holding an eval job's runs (`common.train_lists`).

## 4. Loss and training

```bash
uv run python -m combat.train --list data/CHARACTER/lists/NAME --env SL --name NAME [--test-list LIST]
```

The loss of a sample is (`won_group`, `loss`, `learn/combat/net.py:329-341`)

$$
\text{loss} = \text{CE}(\text{masked endings}, \text{class}) + \text{won} \sum_{\text{held}} \text{BCE}(u_{\text{id},\, g(\text{class})}, \text{gone}),
$$

where $g(\text{class})$ is the group of 10 buckets the win ended in: a win trains its own group's potion chances, and no other group's.

The potions' term has weight 1 (`POTION_WEIGHT`), which is about 2 % of the loss: 0.084 potion rows per won sample at about 0.29 nats, against a cross-entropy of about 1.32. So the body stays the endings'.

The settings are fixed in the code (`learn/combat/train.py:34-36`): AdamW at learning rate 10⁻³ and weight decay 10⁻⁴; batches of 256; at most 20 epochs, ending after 3 without a held-out loss lower by 10⁻⁴. An experiment changes the code, and only a result keeps the change. The token tables are the game's, fixed ([The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2)).

**Going on from a net (`--init FILE`).** Training can start from a net file's weights, read back from its ONNX graph (`export.weights`), at the lower learning rate 3 × 10⁻⁴. The file's weights stand unless an epoch beats their held-out loss. A file with other tables than the game's is refused.

**The report** (`learn/combat/report.py`), one JSON line on stdout, also kept as the net file's `fit`: `env`, `runs`, `held_out_runs`, `samples`, `held_out_samples`, `vocab`, then `held_out` and the same by act, room, encounter, HP (below 30 % or not) and potion id. A sample's potion chances are those of the group of buckets its win ended in (`predict`). With `--test-list`, `medium` holds the same for that whole list, which is reported and never trained on.

| field | what it says |
|---|---|
| `ce`, `ce_prior` | the endings' cross-entropy, and that of the training set's frequencies |
| `death`, `predicted_death` | the means, actual and predicted |
| `rank_lost`, `rank_lost_share` | rank correlation with the HP lost, on wins, within each (encounter, round) of 10 or more, by their sizes; beside it, the HP share's: what a net adds to the HP held |
| `auc_death`, `auc_death_share` | the chance that a death's round start scores above a survivor's within each (encounter, round) with 3 or more of each; beside it, the HP share's |
| `potions` | `rows`, `used`, `predicted`, `log_loss`, and `log_loss_id` and `log_loss_id_room`, those of a rate per id and per id and room |

**The net file** goes to `weights/all/NAME.json` and is never overwritten ([Reference §6](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-6)). Its fields are in [Reference §7](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-7).

## 5. What it measures

The numbers are the report in the champion's file, `weights/all/c9-2.json`. "Held out" is every fifth run of the training list, 4,310 runs of all five characters at ascension 0 with 430,142 round starts, played at the medium budget.

| metric | held out | the HP share alone |
|---|---|---|
| endings' cross-entropy (the prior's) | 1.320 (2.374) | – |
| deaths, actual and predicted | 4.50 %, 4.65 % | – |
| rank of HP lost, on wins | 0.725 | 0.133 |
| AUC of a death | 0.931 | 0.691 |
| potions' log loss (per id and room) | 0.292 (0.465) | – |

**What to read.** The rank of HP lost and the AUC of a death are what the net adds beyond the HP held, so each stands beside the HP share's own. How a run values the endings is V's, not the report's ([Combat brain: the leaf §5](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-5)).

By room, on the held-out runs:

| room | round starts | cross-entropy (prior) | deaths, actual and predicted | rank of HP lost | AUC of a death (HP share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hallway | 268,349 | 0.930 (1.884) | 0.3 %, 0.2 % | 0.710 | 0.978 (0.913) |
| elite | 68,862 | 1.780 (2.798) | 1.7 %, 1.9 % | 0.753 | 0.954 (0.835) |
| boss | 92,931 | 2.106 (3.473) | 18.8 %, 19.4 % | 0.751 | 0.928 (0.674) |

**Low HP**, below 30 % of max HP, where 16.1 % of the held-out round starts die: the AUC of a death is 0.925 against the HP share's 0.547.

**The potion head by id.** On the held-out runs Fairy in a Bottle is used 13.8 % of the time and predicted at 13.3 %, with a log loss of 0.136 against 0.282 per id and room. Across all rows, 27.3 % are used, and 27.4 % predicted.

**The hardest fight.** On Aeonglass, where 42.7 % of the held-out round starts die, the net predicts 45.5 %, and its AUC of a death is 0.933 against 0.626. Its rank of HP lost, on wins, is 0.718 against 0.211.

Which of these translates to more won runs is for a gate to say ([Evaluation: gates §5](/en/arch/evaluation/gates/#sec-5)); the champion's own results are in [Evaluation: champions and baselines §2](/en/arch/evaluation/champions/#sec-2).
