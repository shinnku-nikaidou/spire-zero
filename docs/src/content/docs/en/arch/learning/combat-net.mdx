The combat net is the combat search's learned leaf: from a round's start it predicts how the fight will end under the policy that played its training games. What it reads and how its layers combine that are in [The combat net](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/), and what the search does with its outputs is in [The leaf](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/). This page is about how it is trained, from which data, and what the trained net measures.

## 1. What it predicts

From a round's start, two things:

- **The ending**: a distribution over 51 classes, a death or one of 50 buckets of the share of max HP still to be lost from this state, the buckets the HP held rules out closed.
- **The potions**: after a win, each held potion's chance of being gone.

`learn/combat/` trains it (`combat.train`, `learn/combat/train.py:41`). `--combat-net FILE` plays it, in the SL planner (shown as `plan NAME`) or in PIMC (`pimc NAME`).

## 2. What it reads

Everything is in [The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2). `learn/combat/encode.py` is the one specification, which `CombatNet` follows in C#.

## 3. Samples and labels

`learn/combat/data.py` makes the samples from a list of decision logs and run records ([Reference §4](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-4)).

- **A sample is a round's start**: the first combat decision of each round in the logs, the state a search leaf stands in, since the search's leaves are the starts of the next round ([The leaf §1](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-1)). A combat's decisions close at the next macro decision, or when the round goes back; card-prompt lines are skipped (`combats`, `learn/combat/data.py:33-67`).
- **The enemies' history** before it is rebuilt from the earlier rounds' starts, each living enemy's intents by its uid, as Harness's `Dispatcher` builds it (`witness`, `learn/combat/data.py:70-77`).
- **The ending's label is relative to the state**: a death, or the bucket of the HP share at this state less the share at the fight's end (`ending`, `learn/combat/data.py:97-102`). A heal larger than the lowest bucket falls into it. So every round start of a fight has one end, but its own label. Monte Carlo: it is what really happened under the policy that played ([The loop §3](/en/arch/learning/loop/#sec-3)).
- **A potion's label** comes from the first run view after the fight, per copy: a potion held at this state and absent there is gone (`potions_used`, `learn/combat/data.py:80-90`). Not from the actions, which miss a Fairy in a Bottle spent with no action, 6.2 % of all potions gone. Not from the last round's start, which misses a potion drunk at the last action. A potion gained later in the fight is no potion of this state. A death trains only the ending.
- **What is left out** (`read`, `learn/combat/data.py:131-158`): failed runs; the runs of a seed file given to `--skip-seeds`; a combat with no `end` in its record, unless it is a lost run's last, which counts as a death; a win with no run view after it; and every run whose logged combats do not match its record's, in number or in encounters, which the trainer counts and reports.
- **One environment.** `--env SL|NoSL` reads only that environment's lines; a log line of the other fails the training. The net is trained on `--env SL` ([The leaf §6](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-6)).
- **Weights and split.** Every round start weighs the same, and every fifth run by run id is held out, with all its round starts, for early stopping and the report. One held-out round start in 1,000 keeps its observation and history for the net file's test states (`KEEP`).

**The data** the champion's weights learned from is whole SL runs: 21,263 self-play runs, every combat fought and most of them at the low budget ([The SL planner: budget §2](/en/arch/sl-planner/budget/#sec-2)), plus 600 runs at medium budget on the seeds `promotion-301-500`. That makes 21,863 runs and 1,625,608 round starts, of which 325,309 are held out. The seeds of `promotion-1-300` are never trained on.

Of the wins' round starts, the mask never closes the true bucket, and 98 of 1,479,991 heal beyond the lowest bucket. 9.4 % of the round starts hold a potion.

## 4. Loss and training

```bash
uv run python -m combat.train --list data/silent/lists/c7 --env SL --name NAME --skip-seeds ../eval/seeds/promotion-1-300.txt --test-list data/silent/lists/medium
```

The loss of a sample is (`loss`, `learn/combat/net.py:300-307`)

$$
\text{loss} = \text{CE}(\text{masked endings}, \text{class}) + \text{won} \sum_{\text{held}} \text{BCE}(u_{\text{id}}, \text{gone}).
$$

The potions' term has weight 1 (`POTION_WEIGHT`), which is about 4 % of the loss: 0.105 potion rows per won sample at about 0.46 nats, against a cross-entropy of about 1.27. So the body stays the endings'.

The settings are fixed in the code (`learn/combat/train.py:35-37`): AdamW at learning rate 10⁻³ and weight decay 10⁻⁴; batches of 256; at most 20 epochs, ending after 3 without a held-out loss lower by 10⁻⁴. An experiment changes the code, and only a result keeps the change. The token tables are the game's, fixed ([The combat net §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/network/#sec-2)). The best epoch of the training behind the champion's weights was 5 of 8, in 1 h 51 min on a GPU.

**Going on from a net (`--init FILE`).** Training can start from a net file's weights, read back from its ONNX graph (`export.weights`), at the lower learning rate 3 × 10⁻⁴. The file's weights stand unless an epoch beats their held-out loss. A file with other tables than the game's is refused.

**The report** (`learn/combat/report.py`), one JSON line on stdout, also kept as the net file's `fit`: `env`, `runs`, `held_out_runs`, `samples`, `held_out_samples`, `vocab`, then `held_out` and the same by act, room, encounter, HP (below 30 % or not) and potion id. With `--test-list`, `medium` holds the same for the runs of `--skip-seeds`' seeds in that list, which are reported and never trained on.

| field | what it says |
|---|---|
| `ce`, `ce_prior` | the endings' cross-entropy, and that of the training set's frequencies |
| `death`, `predicted_death` | the means, actual and predicted |
| `rank_within`, `rank_within_share` | rank correlation with the end's price within each (encounter, round) of 10 or more, by their sizes; beside it, the HP share's |
| `rank_lost`, `rank_lost_share` | the same with the HP lost, on wins: what a net adds to the HP held |
| `auc_death`, `auc_death_share` | the chance that a death's round start scores above a survivor's within each (encounter, round) with 3 or more of each; beside it, the HP share's |
| `potions` | `rows`, `used`, `predicted`, `log_loss`, and `log_loss_id` and `log_loss_id_room`, those of a rate per id and per id and room |

**The net file** goes to `weights/CHARACTER/NAME.json` and is never overwritten ([Reference §6](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-6)). Its fields are in [Reference §7](/en/arch/learning/reference/#sec-7).

## 5. What it measures

The numbers are the report in `weights/silent/c8-1.json`, from the training its weights come from, which did not read `damages` or `orb_values`. "Held out" is every fifth run of the training list, mostly played at the low budget. "Medium" is 39,732 round starts of runs at medium budget on the seeds `promotion-1-300`, which it never trained on.

| metric | held out | medium | the HP share alone (held out, medium) |
|---|---|---|---|
| endings' cross-entropy (the prior's) | 1.266 (2.148) | 1.344 (2.118) | – |
| deaths, actual and predicted | 5.59 %, 5.39 % | 4.02 %, 4.42 % | – |
| rank with the end's price | 0.926 | 0.896 | 0.819, 0.796 |
| rank of HP lost, on wins | 0.634 | 0.600 | 0.082, 0.087 |
| AUC of a death | 0.942 | 0.874 | 0.758, 0.684 |
| potions' log loss (per id and room) | 0.311 (0.478) | 0.359 (0.487) | – |

**What to read.** The rank with the end's price says little: the HP share alone gets most of it, since the end's price follows the HP held. The rank of HP lost and the AUC of a death are what the net adds beyond the HP held.

By room, on the held-out runs:

| room | round starts | cross-entropy (prior) | deaths, actual and predicted | rank of HP lost | AUC of a death (HP share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hallway | 256,011 | 1.089 (1.811) | 1.7 %, 1.6 % | 0.607 | 0.960 (0.851) |
| elite | 23,476 | 1.841 (2.869) | 9.6 %, 8.7 % | 0.738 | 0.941 (0.777) |
| boss | 45,822 | 1.956 (3.661) | 25.1 %, 24.8 % | 0.775 | 0.935 (0.719) |

**Low HP**, below 30 % of max HP, where 21.5 % of the held-out round starts die: the AUC of a death is 0.904 against the HP share's 0.633, and on the medium runs 0.960 against 0.510.

**The potion head by id.** On the held-out runs Fairy in a Bottle is used 13.3 % of the time and predicted at 13.1 %, with a log loss of 0.141 against 0.355 per id and room. Across all rows, 23.1 % are used, and 22.7 % predicted.

**The hardest fight.** On Test Subject, where 91.2 % of the held-out round starts die, the net predicts 91.4 %, and its AUC of a death is 0.854 against 0.613. There are too few wins there to measure anything about HP lost.

Which of these translates to more won runs is for a gate to say ([Evaluation: gates §5](/en/arch/evaluation/gates/#sec-5)). On the 150 seeds its gate finished, it won 88 runs, as many as an earlier net on the same data ([Evaluation: champions and baselines §2](/en/arch/evaluation/champions/#sec-2)).
