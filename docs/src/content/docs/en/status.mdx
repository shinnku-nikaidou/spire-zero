## 2. Main gaps and risks

1. **CombatBuilder approximations.** Turn history, internal counters, durations of cost modifiers and stun follow-ups are approximated, and the builder has never been checked against observations from the real game ([Simulator: combats §6](/en/arch/simulator/combat/#sec-6)).
2. **Monster move resampling** picks uniformly among moves that show the same intent. It needs a posterior built from the monster's history ([Harness §4](/en/arch/harness/#sec-4)).
3. **The card prompt context relies on Harmony prefixes over async entries:** inlining or a rejit can bypass them ([GameBridge: reading the game §6](/en/arch/gamebridge/observe/#sec-6), [Mod: auto-play and safety §5](/en/arch/mod/auto/#sec-5)).
4. **Run start uses `UnlockState.all`,** not the user's profile, and ascension is not clamped to the character's maximum ([Simulator: whole runs §2](/en/arch/simulator/runs/#sec-2)).
5. **New game code that touches Godot nodes** without the TestMode guard, outside events (where the transpiler covers it), shows up only as game errors in `run` batches ([Simulator: headless host §4](/en/arch/simulator/headless/#sec-4)).
6. **Harmony patches in the real game vs tiered JIT:** the runtime may recompile a method and silently drop a patch ([Mod: auto-play and safety §5](/en/arch/mod/auto/#sec-5)).
7. **Combat brain v0 blind spots.** Card prompts are answered by a fixed rule (`p0`), both inside search and in the real combat, not searched; NoSL's search sees this turn only; SL's planner sees the whole combat but proves few of its plans; an unproved loss gets at most 3 plans in a row by default, the third played to its end, so a combat that only a later plan would win is lost ([The SL planner: beam §6](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-6), [The SL planner: beam §5](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-5)); the leaf is hand-made and ignores most powers ([Combat brain: the leaf §2](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-2)).
8. **The gauntlet's V in whole runs.** V is trained where plain monster combats are free, so it values routes and rest sites for that game. About half of v6-3-2's losses in whole runs come between two bosses, and the gauntlet's order of two nets did not always hold in whole runs ([Learning §2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2), [§8](/en/arch/learning/#sec-8)).
9. **V's labels carry the later draws of their runs,** and V is not told a run's ε ([Learning §5](/en/arch/learning/#sec-5)).

## 3. Implementation order

<Steps>

1. <Badge text="done 2026-10-26" variant="success" /> Run session with every surface headless, using a placeholder macro policy; coverage, determinism and cross-process map tests ([Simulator: whole runs §4](/en/arch/simulator/runs/#sec-4), [Simulator: reference §7](/en/arch/simulator/reference/#sec-7)).

2. <Badge text="done 2026-10-26" variant="success" /> Protocol run-level types and `ChoiceContext`; GameBridge observe and legal for every surface.

3. <Badge text="done 2026-10-26" variant="success" /> The combat world API in process, combat brain v0 (PIMC with a v0 leaf value) and the combat panel; v0 beats the placeholders on every pool ([NoSL PIMC: budget §6](/en/arch/pimc/budget/#sec-6)).

4. <Badge text="done 2026-10-27" variant="success" /> Worker pool and IPC: PIMC's NoSL worlds are searched in worker processes beside the process that holds the run, and the combat brain plays the combats of whole runs ([Simulator: the worker pool §2](/en/arch/simulator/pool/#sec-2), [NoSL PIMC: budget §5](/en/arch/pimc/budget/#sec-5)).

5. <Badge text="done 2026-10-27" variant="success" /> Harness dispatch: routing, brain seeds and the decision log, for every run ([Harness: routing, budgets and seeds §1](/en/arch/harness/routing/#sec-1)–[Harness: the decision log §1](/en/arch/harness/decision-log/#sec-1)). The public record comes with the first inference that needs it ([Harness §4](/en/arch/harness/#sec-4)).

6. Macro brain L0 and a v0 value function; R1/R2 full runs; the evaluation pipeline. L0 v0, V v0, R1 full runs and the pipeline are done, with the first reports in `eval/silent/reports/` ([Evaluation §6](/en/arch/evaluation/#sec-6)); R2 is left.

7. Mod: the executor and the auto-run driver, giving the R4 pipeline; the R3 ≡ R4 equivalence test. Loading, the brain link and the assist panel on every surface are done, in both environments, with the SL export and its round trip ([Mod §3](/en/arch/mod/#sec-3)–[the SL record](/en/arch/mod/sl-record/), [§8](/en/arch/mod/sl-record/#sec-8)).

8. SL worlds in the worker pool, loaded from the clone; the SL leaderboard. The node checkpoint, the SL clone, its seal, SL worlds in runs and the SL planner, which plans whole combats on the clone in the worker pool, are done ([Simulator: worlds and the SL clone §3](/en/arch/simulator/worlds/#sec-3), [Simulator: the worker pool §3](/en/arch/simulator/pool/#sec-3), [The SL planner §3](/en/arch/sl-planner/#sec-3)); the SL leaderboard had its first champion, set by its baseline on 2026-10-28: the SL planner c2 with L0 n2-b0.03, floor 28.25 on `promotion-1`. Since 2026-10-30 its champion is the SL planner c2 with L0 v6-3-2 on the clone: floor 33.56 and 23 of 300 won on `promotion-1-300` ([Evaluation §7](/en/arch/evaluation/#sec-7)).

9. The learning loop (fit V, ExIt, SPSA, gates), then the LLM patch loop. Fitting V from self-play, SPSA on V's and the leaf's weights, the full-run gate, with a champion file per environment, the elite and boss budget multiples ([NoSL PIMC: budget §4](/en/arch/pimc/budget/#sec-4)), and the ExIt data in the decision log and run records ([Harness: the decision log §5](/en/arch/harness/decision-log/#sec-5)) are done ([Learning §2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2), [Evaluation §5](/en/arch/evaluation/#sec-5)). So are V v2 (the macro V net) and its training, and exploration for self-play ([Learning §1](/en/arch/learning/#sec-1), [§2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2)), moved ahead of the combat items because card picks were the weakest part. The combat outcome head is built too, as the combat net, PIMC's learned leaf, which reads the enemies' intents in the rounds so far ([Learning §1](/en/arch/learning/#sec-1)). The combat net's first generation passed the gate (c2, [NoSL PIMC: budget §6](/en/arch/pimc/budget/#sec-6)), and self-play's second generation runs with it, exploring by value ([Learning §5](/en/arch/learning/#sec-5)). V v2's first promoted generation is n2-b0.03 ([Learning §2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2)). Pure V replaced V v2, first trained on whole runs (pure-1-g), then in the elite gauntlet with even draws for exploration; its v6-3-2 is both champions' V, the first V whose runs win ([Learning §2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2), [§5](/en/arch/learning/#sec-5)).

   Next: V's training and gate move back to whole SL runs, with every combat fought; V decides by P(win) and is gated on paired wins; and V gets a build encoder (its cards, relics and potions as one set with attention) and a route encoder (the map's paths) inside the same net, with one body and one value. NoSL's items (the combat net's next generation through the gate, tuning K and nodes per world, the fusion panel's measurement, sequential halving at the root, the prior net, then the closed loop with PCR and Reanalyse) wait while SL comes first ([Learning §2](/en/arch/learning/#sec-2), [NoSL PIMC: budget §7](/en/arch/pimc/budget/#sec-7), [Combat brain: the leaf §6](/en/arch/combat-brain/leaf/#sec-6)).
