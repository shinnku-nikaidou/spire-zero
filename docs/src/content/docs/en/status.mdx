This page tracks progress. The chapters say how each part works; this page says how far along each part is, what could still go wrong, and what comes next. As of 2026-10-31.

## 1. In one paragraph

The AI plays whole Silent A0 runs headless, in the game's own code, in both environments. In **SL** the champion, the SL planner with the combat net c2 in combat and L0 with V v6-3-2 outside it, wins **7.7%** of runs (23 of 300 on `promotion-1-300`) and reaches floor 33.6 of 48 on average; before plan v6's elite gauntlet no V had won a run. In **NoSL** no champion has won a run yet. In the real game the mod's assist mode answers every decision screen in both environments; auto-play is not built. The work now is plan v7: V trained and gated on whole SL runs again, deciding by P(win), with a build and a route encoder inside the net.

## 3. Main gaps and risks

1. **CombatBuilder approximations.** Turn history, internal counters, durations of cost modifiers and stun follow-ups are approximated, and the builder has never been checked against observations from the real game ([Simulator: combats §6](/en/arch/simulator/combat/#sec-6)).
2. **Monster move resampling** picks uniformly among moves that show the same intent; a posterior from the monster's history would be better ([Harness §4](/en/arch/harness/#sec-4)).
3. **The card prompt context relies on Harmony prefixes over async entries**: inlining or a rejit can bypass them ([GameBridge: reading the game §6](/en/arch/gamebridge/observe/#sec-6), [Mod: auto-play and safety §5](/en/arch/mod/auto/#sec-5)).
4. **Run start uses `UnlockState.all`**, not the player's profile, and ascension is not clamped to the character's maximum ([Simulator: whole runs §2](/en/arch/simulator/runs/#sec-2)).
5. **New game code that touches Godot nodes** without the TestMode guard, outside events (where the transpiler covers it), shows up only as game errors in `run` batches ([Simulator: headless host §4](/en/arch/simulator/headless/#sec-4)).
6. **Harmony patches in the real game against tiered JIT**: the runtime may recompile a method and silently drop a patch ([Mod: auto-play and safety §5](/en/arch/mod/auto/#sec-5)).
7. **The combat brains' blind spots.** Card prompts are answered by a fixed rule (`p0`), not searched; NoSL's search sees this turn only; the SL planner proves few of its plans, and an unproved loss gets at most 3 plans in a row, so a combat that only a later plan would win is lost ([The SL planner: beam §5](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-5)–[§6](/en/arch/sl-planner/beam/#sec-6)).
8. **V's training game.** v6-3-2 was trained where plain monster combats are free; about half of its losses in whole runs come between two bosses, and the gauntlet's order of two nets did not always hold in whole runs. Plan v7 trains on whole runs again ([Learning: the experiment log §9](/en/arch/learning/experiments/#sec-9)).
9. **V's labels carry the later exploration draws of their runs**, and V is not told a run's ε ([Learning: the loop §9](/en/arch/learning/loop/#sec-9)).
10. **SL in the real game without the node's clone**: the mod holds back only combat decisions, so a brain whose macro side plays on the clone would use the previous node's ([Mod: the SL record §6](/en/arch/mod/sl-record/#sec-6)).
