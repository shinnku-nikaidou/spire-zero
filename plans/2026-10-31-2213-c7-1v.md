## Why

- c2 on SL round starts: held-out CE 2.086 against a prior's 2.147; medium 3.264 against 2.119; medium bosses predict death 80.3 %, actual 18.4 %; rank within (encounter, round) 0.814 held-out, 0.696 medium, 0.470 medium bosses, Test Subject 0.016 / 0.206. On medium it does not know 21.6 % of the enemies, 23.7 % of the relics, 28.9 % of the modifiers.
- Potions are priced by count at a flat 0.02, gold and lost cards not at all, while what a potion is worth depends on the deck (the user). The net should learn worth as the macro net does. Bots hold 0.1 potions per round start; whether the flat price causes that is not shown.

## The net

**Input**, a round's start, `learn/combat/encode.py` with: added current cost (`costs`), modifier amounts (`mod_amounts`), relic counters and status (`relic_values`, `relic_status`), all fed by C# today; gold stays (the user: the state is described in the input); act, room and character go (the vocabulary lists only encounters in `context`, so they read as `<unk>`, no C# change). Lagged intents stay. Vocabulary rebuilt from training; no id dropout (0 unknown on the medium test set).

**Output**, one number, no buckets, no potion head:

```
label = V(the first run_view after the fight) / V(the last run_view before it), capped; a death = 0; a run won by this fight: V = 1
```

**Loss**: squared error. Every round start weighs the same. Every fifth run by run id held out.

**In the planner**, all in V's units:

- in progress: the net's number x V before the fight, kept from the combat's first plan
- won: V of the run with the HP and potions it ends with
- lost: 0
- the bound: the same as won, the most at that HP or less, as `PricedLeaf.Of` does

The route stays `--leaf-prices v`'s: the host makes the prices at each plan and sends them to the workers, so workers need no V. `EndPrices` becomes a table of V by HP per subset of the held potions; `Death` and the flat `Potion` go. Gold and the deck in those prices are the run's at the decision.
