## A. The pool: the pool plan with this morning's changes

Everything in the pool plan holds, except:

1. **Order between steps**: strictly by priority (self-play 0, eval and forks 1), then launch order. A gate launched
   during v9g2 takes every thread as its run ends; v9g2 goes on once the gate's queues are empty.
2. **Inside a step**: the job with the lowest share of its queue dealt (dealt / queue length; ties: launch order), in
   place of "fewest dealt". For a gate's ten equal jobs it is the same rule, so the two pairs stay on the same seeds;
   for v9g2's 165 jobs of 18 to 1,000 seeds it keeps the mix of part B at every moment, so a deadline cut leaves the
   planned proportions.
3. **Deadlines** (the user): a gate has none. It ends when its queue is used up and every seed it dealt is back.
   Self-play and forks keep one (`DEADLINE=NAME`, editable with `box.py deadline`, extendable after it passes until the
   step is fetched). The pool plan's deadline guard (median play time per host) is dropped: with no deadline on gates,
   it would only save a data step's last runs, at most one per thread.
4. **Seeds go back to the hub** (the user: cut any host by force; what is left waits on the hub; a host that comes
   back takes work again). A seed dealt but not finished goes back to the front of its job's queue when
   - its run is killed by a cut: `box.py down HOST`, `box.py threads HOST N` below the threads in play, or the end of a
     threads window (pushqkzh at 06:00 cuts to 4 at once: the rule is kept to the minute, and nothing is lost);
   - the host's worker has not been heard from for 15 min (a crash, a reboot, a lost network). If it comes back, any
     chunk it sends for a seed taken back is refused, so no seed is counted twice;
   - the host's worker starts again (it holds nothing then).
   Not dealt again: a run that ended in a game error (dropped, as now, and two in a row refuse that host the job), and
   seeds in play when a self-play step's deadline passes (the step is over). With every host down the queues wait on
   the hub: a gate until hosts are back, a self-play step until its deadline. `box.py lost` goes (the 15-min rule
   covers it); a lost answer to `/next` is still dealt again to the same thread.
5. **Threads** (each host's only knob, `box.py threads`): gcpsg 32, pushqkzh `19:00-06:00 13` and 4 the rest of the
   day, ds6754 15, pusheen 10, cshinnku 5, hostc 5, ovh 3, local 0 (no large data generation here since
   2026-11-03). The Colab notebooks join self-play steps with 3 threads each and `--until-empty`.

The test (the pool plan's §7) adds: `box.py down` on one host mid-run (its seeds go to other hosts at once); every
worker down (the queues wait), then two back up (they go on); a worker killed with SIGKILL (its seed dealt again after
15 min; its late chunk refused when it comes back); a threads window that ends during the test (its threads cut, their
seeds back); a gate-like step with no deadline (it ends when whole, the pairs on the same seeds); a step with jobs of
unequal size cut by its deadline (each job's share dealt within one seed of the others').

## B. v9g2

### B.1 What the nets know of ascension (measured this morning)

Both nets read the ascension (V: one of six raw state numbers, scaled by the file's mean 0 and std 1; the combat net:
ascension / 10 among its extra inputs). What they learned of it comes only from the Silent's A10 data (v7g2, v8g1, v8g2,
through v8-3, carried with no training to v9-0 and c9-0). v9-1, v9-2, c9-1 and c9-2 were trained on A0 alone, which
gives that input no gradient: V's ascension column has the same norm in v9-0 and v9-2 (0.5628), the combat net's moves
0.4985 → 0.4974 (weight decay). No net has seen A1-A9, nor any other character above A0. On v9-2's eight test
decisions (A0, four characters) setting the ascension to 10 moves V's win chance by +0.02 to +0.11 (0.234 → 0.347),
always upward, and by the same for every option of a decision; so A1-A10 are played much as A0, plus the Silent's old
A10 offset. v9g2 is the first data that can teach both nets the ascension for every character. Nothing is changed in the
nets for it.

### B.2 The runs

0.02, a third each, uniform draws only, the same at every ascension (v9g1's setting, so the ascension is the only thing
