# c7-2: SL's combat net, by how the fight ends and which potions go

For the user to approve (Taiwan time 2026-11-01 01:15). Nothing is built, trained or gated yet. c7-1's and c7-1v's results were not in when this was written, and the numbers against c7-1 below are to be filled from its report.

Measures: read-only over v7g1 on pushqkzh, 1 process, nice 19, no GPU; scripts and `out/` in `~/sts2/tmp/c72`, the raw output in side-session-notes `local_2bede074-c6c4-47fd-835f-89d625a45d97/c7-2-measures.md`. 21,263 runs read, 12 failed ones dropped, 0 mismatched; 302,084 fights, 281,214 won; 1,567,874 round starts.

## The net in one paragraph

c7-1's inputs and data, one body, two heads. **Endings**: 51 classes as c2, with the buckets a win cannot reach masked inside the graph. **Potions**: one chance per potion id of being gone by the end of a win. The value is the hand prices, `LeafValue`'s. **No gold head and no max HP head in this net** (decision 2, the user's to overrule).

## What this net can and cannot change

This must be plain before approving it.

- Against c7-1 only two things change: the mask and the potion term. The planner's bound and every finished line are the same.
- At a flat 0.02 a potion is at most 2.3 HP at 70 max HP (0.02 / 0.6 x 70), whichever fight it is. **No head can make the planner save a potion for a boss at that price**; only prices can. The heads learn when potions go; the flat price does not use which potion it is, only the sum.
- So a flat gate is the likely outcome and would not judge the design. What this net shows is whether the potion head predicts by id and fight, which is what later prices would read.

## 3. The potion head's shape

**`nn.Linear(BODY, len(vocab["potions"]))` then a sigmoid: output `potion_used`, shape `[batch, ids]`, one chance per id in the potions table.** Width fixed by the file, so no new dynamic axis. Training reads the held ones with `gather` over the `potions` input and its mask; C# reads them with the `Index("potions", id)` it has; two copies of an id read the same chance, and their labels (1, 0 when one went) train it to 0.5, whose sum is right.

An output per slot through an id embedding was the other way: a variable-length output for a head with 52 ids and 0.1 potions per round start, and it shares nothing the body does not already see. Rejected.

## 4. Losses, and c7-1v's failure

**Where c7-1v's target varies.** Its label is one number for all of a fight's round starts: inside a fight it does not vary at all. On the 9-shard test (17,508 fights): on wins variance 0.282, the encounter explains 36 %, and within an encounter HP lost correlates -0.276 with it (7.6 % of what is left), potions used -0.009. V before the fight has median 0.0045 and is below 0.02 in 87 % of fights, so the label is a ratio of small numbers. The net could only learn the fight it was in, and nothing told it that HP held now counts.

**Why endings differ.** c2's label is also one outcome per fight: of the HP lost from here, 46 % of the variance is left after (encounter, round), 11.4 % after the fight too, and that 11.4 % is mechanical (within a fight the end is fixed, so the label moves with HP held: correlation -0.993). What saves it is the form:

> value = what the state holds, exactly + what the net says is still to come

HP held enters exactly, so siblings in a beam differ by their true HP before the net says anything. **The rule for every head: it predicts a change from the state; what the state holds counts in full.** The potion head obeys it: a potion drunk leaves the sum exactly.

Logs hold one line per fight, so no label here separates sibling states. That is a limit of round-start data, not something a loss can fix.

**The losses.**

```
loss = CE(masked endings, class) + POTION_WEIGHT x won x sum over held potions of BCE(chance of its id, gone)
```

`POTION_WEIGHT = 1`. Measured sizes: 0.105 potion rows per win row x about 0.46 nats = about 0.05, against a CE of about 1.27: 4 % of the loss, so the body stays the endings'. c7-1's term is 0.1 x a squared error of about 0.03: 0.2 %. Every round start weighs the same; every fifth run held out; early stopping on the total. No weight experiments.

**The mask** is a game rule: a win ends with `0 < share <= 1`, so `lost < share` and `lost >= share - 1`. A bucket `[low, low + w)` stays if `low < share` and `low + w > share - 1 - 1e-6`. On 1,479,991 won round starts the truth is never masked (0 and 0), 98 heals are clamped into bucket 0, and that bucket is always open for them. Buckets open per state: mean 36.6, least 11. On win rows c2 puts 1.98 % of its weight there, 16.4 % below 30 % HP (7.4 % of the rows, where 21.3 % die).

## 5. The value in the planner

```
in progress:  sum_k>=1 p_k (0.2 + 0.6 (s - m_k)) + (1 - p_0) x 0.02 x sum_held (1 - u_id)
won, lost:    LeafValue, unchanged
bound:        LeafValue of a win at the state's HP and potions, unchanged
```

| part | what it changes |
|---|---|
| pruning, `Proved`, `_best` | nothing: `CombatNet.Value` sends any finished status and every bound to `LeafValue.Of` (`CombatNet.cs:97`, `SlPlanner.cs:325`) |
| the beam's order and `keep` | the only place the heads act; also the tie among losing lines (`from`) and a line stopped at `MaxTurns` |
| mask | near death: a win's term stays above 0.19 (a bucket's middle may pass the HP held by half a width), so impossible buckets no longer pull a state toward a death's 0 |
| potion term | bounded in [0, 0.02 q]. A count can leave it: c2's head is below 0 on 46.7 % of win rows, and its squared error 0.0390 is worse than the mean's 0.0255. A potion the net expects gone no longer pays 0.02 for being kept: keeping and drinking differ by the HP, not by a bonus the state would spend anyway |
| size | 0.02 x used has std 0.0091 on the 9.4 % of rows holding a potion; 0.6 x HP lost 0.074 there. At most 2.3 HP per potion |
| gold | no term, here or on a finished line; no effect |
| max HP | no term but through `share`; no effect. The Cheese leaves `share` almost unmoved; Paper Cuts and Brightest Flame stay uncharged |

The value can pass the bound only by predicted healing, as today (1.84 % of won round starts healed at all), and at full HP the mask forbids it.

## 6. The code

### Python

`learn/combat/encode.py`: nothing.

`learn/combat/data.py`
- `potions_used(obs, after) -> list[int]`: per held potion in slot order, 0 while a copy is left in `after["potions"]`, else 1.
- `sample(...)`: takes `after`; `used` and `held` go; adds `potion_used` (per held potion, on a win; `[0] * held` on a death) and `lost` (share now less share at the end, on a win).
- `read(...)`: passes `fight["after"]`; a win with no view after is left out (0 in v7g1).
- `edges()`: the buckets' bottoms, and the width, from `BUCKETS` and `LOSS_MIN`.

`learn/combat/net.py`
- `Batch`: `self.potions`, `self.potion_mask` (from `bags`), `self.potion_used` of the same width; `self.used` goes.
- `Net.__init__`: `self.potion_head = nn.Linear(BODY, sizes["potions"])`; a buffer of the bottoms.
- `Net.mask(logits, player)`: the rule of §4, `torch.where` on `player[:, 0:1]` to -1e4; class 0 open; applied in `forward`, so the graph holds it and C# holds nothing of it.
- `Outcome.forward`: softmax, sigmoid.
- `loss`: §4; `POTION_WEIGHT = 1`.
- The worth path stays until c7-1v's gate is read; removing it is its own commit.

`learn/combat/train.py`
- `evaluate`: the value by §5, `(1 - sigmoid(gather)) * mask`.
- `auc_within(samples, score, key)`: AUC within (encounter, round), by deaths.
- `part(ix)` adds `rank_lost` (predicted against actual HP lost, wins), `rank_lost_share` and `auc_death_share` (the HP-share baselines), `auc_death`, and `potions`: log loss against the id and id + room rates, mean used against mean predicted, by id.
- `--test-list`: logs read like the others and reported as `medium`, none trained on, `--skip-seeds` not applied to them: the gate seeds' v7-1f 100 and v7-1 300, 39,549 round starts.

`learn/combat/export.py`
- `onnx_bytes`: outputs `outcome`, `potion_used`; `AXES` gets `potion_used: {0: "batch"}`.
- `net_file`: `target: "each_potion"`, `buckets`, `loss_min`; each test `{obs, history, outcome, potion_used}`. A pick with a held potion and one below 30 % HP among the 16, next to the multi-hit one.

**The net file**: `name, character, env, target, buckets, loss_min, lags, round, vocab, fit, onnx, tests`. **The graph**: inputs `encode.INPUTS` unchanged; outputs `outcome [batch, 51]` (masked classes are exactly 0), `potion_used [batch, ids]`.

`learn/tests/test_combat.py`: `potions_used` (a last-action drink, Fairy gone with no action, a duplicate, a gain ignored); the truth is never masked and masked classes are 0 at 5 % and at 100 % HP; `test_the_onnx_graph...` with both outputs; `test_training_writes...` with the new fields.

## 7. How to judge it

**Held-out, by set (v7g1 held out, medium), act, room, encounter, against c2's numbers below and c7-1's report.** `rank_within` against the end's price alone says little: **HP share now gets 0.8186 on it, c2 0.8144.** What a net adds over the HP held is what to read:

| metric | HP share alone | c2, v7g1 held out | c2, medium |
|---|---|---|---|
| value's rank against the end's price | 0.8186 (medium 0.797) | 0.8144 | 0.696 |
| rank of HP lost, wins | 0.083 (medium 0.089) | 0.300 | 0.257 |
| AUC of a death within (encounter, round) | 0.758 (medium 0.690) | 0.710 | 0.647 |
| CE against the prior | – | 2.086 / 2.147 | 3.264 / 2.119 |

Potion head: log loss below 0.4606, the id + room rate; mean used against mean predicted; Fairy and the top ids apart.

Mask: read at rows below 30 % HP, AUC of a death and value there.

**The gate**: `--combat-net weights/silent/c7-2.json` with the SL champion's other options, clone, no explore, no V prices. 300 at medium and 300 at low on promotion-1-300; the verdict reads the medium: `wins_lower_95` above 0 (a pair's SE about 3.15 points on 300, about +16 wins), against the champion of that time, whose runs on those seeds exist or are added in the same box. About 75 min on 51 slots.

| result | reading |
|---|---|
| metrics better than c7-1, gate passes | the mask matters where deaths are |
| metrics better, gate flat | expected (see the top): the hand prices do not use what the heads learned |
| AUC of a death still below HP share | the net does not read danger yet: no head will mend it |
| potion log loss not below 0.4606 | the head adds nothing to id and room: reconsider it |
| worse than c7-1 | the two changes are small enough to read apart: look at low HP first |
